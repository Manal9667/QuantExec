--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Strategy performance varies with:
  • Market conditions (price volatility, trending)
  • Order size (small orders have higher fill rates)
  • Execution window (longer windows allow more passive fills)
  • Available liquidity (changes by time and symbol)
  • Assumptions (fees, latency, tick sizes, market impact)

These results demonstrate behavior under ONE set of historical
conditions. Do not generalize to other markets, times, or assumptions.
